RULES: BOXES

RULES: LIGHT EM' UP

  • Place lights on empty cells. A light illuminates every empty cell in the same row and column until blocked by a wall.
  • Numbered walls: the number indicates exactly how many lights must be placed in the four adjacent cells (up, down, left, right). The count must match.
  • No two lights can see each other: if a light would illuminate another light, both cells turn red (invalid).
  • Solve the puzzle when every non‑wall cell is lit, all numbered walls are satisfied, and no lights conflict.

RULES: HINTWORDS

  • Three puzzles per day, each with two clue words. Find the single word that connects them.
  • Connections can be ideas, categories, associations, or compound words.
  • Type your answer and submit. You must solve each puzzle to unlock the next one.
  • Wrong guess: -1 point, reveals first letter of answer, that letter cannot be erased.
  • Lose 1 point every 15 seconds.
