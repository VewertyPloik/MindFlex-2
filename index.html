RULES: SEARCH

  • Locate the target words hidden in the 8x8 data grid.
  • Words can be structured horizontally, vertically, diagonally, forwards, or backwards.
  • Scoring: 50/50 Time and Moves. Lose 1 point every 15 seconds, and 1 point for every incorrect drag.

RULES: TOWERS

  • Each tower has a target number underneath it (1-4).
  • Move blocks so that every tower holds exactly the number of blocks indicated.
  • Tap a tower to select its top block, then tap another tower to move it there.
  • Scoring: 1 point lost per 6 moves and per 5 seconds.

RULES: FIND IT

  • Tap a tile to reveal what's underneath. Your goal is to find the GREEN tile.
  • Yellow: target is 1 tile away (diagonals included).
  • Red: target is 2 tiles away.
  • Black: target is 3 or more tiles away.
  • Tiles flip back after 1 second. Memorize the heat map!
  • Scoring: 1 point lost per 5 moves and per 5 seconds.

RULES: COLOR FLOOD

Your goal is to turn the entire grid into a single color.

  • You always start from the top-left group of tiles.
  • Tap a color on the palette to change your group's color and absorb neighbors.
  • Scoring: Heavily prioritizes minimum moves (90%) over time (10%).

RULES: VALVES

  • Tap to rotate a valve tile 90°.
  • Connect all tiles to each other, running from the main source.
  • Leave ZERO open leaks in the system.

RULES: BOXES

Fill the grid with rectangular boxes so that each box contains exactly ONE number, and its area equals that number.

  • Drag over the grid to create a box.
  • The area number can be anywhere inside the box.
  • Tap an existing box to delete it.

RULES: LIGHT EM' UP

  • Tap an open cell to place or remove a radiant light.
  • A light illuminates every open cell in its row/column until blocked.
  • Numbered walls require exactly that many lights in the four adjacent cells.
  • Puzzle is solved when all open cells are lit, and no lights shine on each other.
